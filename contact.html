100% Purity & Single-Press Standard

Millacoco coconut oil is extracted exclusively from sun-dried Kuttiyadi copra. We guarantee zero mineral oil, zero preservatives, zero liquid paraffin, and zero heat/chemical refining.

Natural Physical Behavior
