Our Heritage & Craft

Rooted in Kuttiyadi Copra,
Bottled Pure in Kozhikode

Millacoco began with a single unshakeable promise: to bring pure, unadulterated cold-pressed coconut oil from Kerala’s lush coastal groves directly to your kitchen and wellness rituals, without additives, sulphur, or shortcuts.

The Kuttiyadi Legacy

Kerala’s Richest Coconuts, Sun-Dried to Perfection

Kuttiyadi coconuts are world-renowned for their dense white copra and sweet, intense natural oil content. We partner directly with local farmers who handpick fully matured coconuts, dry them naturally under the Kerala sun, and carefully grade every single piece before milling.
